# 🌍 Global Ledger: World Economy Simulator

**Project Status:** Concept / Planning  
**Platform:** Android
**Genre:** Hybrid-Casual / Simulation / Tycoon  

## 📖 Overview
**Global Ledger** is a high-stakes global finance simulation designed for mobile. Players take on the role of a "World Controller," managing interest rates, trade policies, and industrial investments to build a thriving global empire—or watch it collapse under the weight of hyperinflation.

The game focuses on the **Butterfly Effect**: a decision made in one region ripples through the global supply chain, affecting prices, sentiment, and stability across the world.

---

## 🚀 Key Pillars

1. **The Butterfly Effect Engine:** A complex mathematical backend where every variable (GDP, Inflation, Debt) is interconnected across global regions.
2. **Edutainment at Scale:** Making macroeconomics "juicy" and addictive through tactile UI, sliders, and immediate visual feedback.
3. **Live World Events:** A dynamic world that reacts to seasonal challenges like "The Green Energy Shift" or "The AI Gold Rush."

---

## 🎮 Player Roles

Players select a specialized role that changes their starting resources and available "Action Cards":

| Role | Primary Objective | Key Power |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Central Banker** | Market Stability | Control over Interest Rates & Money Printing. |
| **The Hedge Fund Mogul** | Maximum Profit | Ability to "Short" markets and trigger rallies. |
| **The Tech Visionary** | Industry Disruption | High R&D speed to replace old industries with Tech. |

## 🛠 Technical Roadmap

### Phase 1: The Engine (Month 1)
- [ ] **Global Data Structure:** Define the JSON/ScriptableObject architecture for regional stats.
- [ ] **The "Tick" System:** Develop the logic loop that calculates economic changes every 5 seconds.
- [ ] **Graybox UI:** Build a functional prototype to test the math before adding art.

### Phase 2: The Visuals (Month 2)
- [ ] **Isometric Diorama:** Create the 3D world map that visually reacts to economic status.
- [ ] **Bento UI System:** Design a clean, tiled interface for Android touchscreens.
- [ ] **Juice & Polish:** Implement haptic feedback, particle effects for booms/crashes, and transitions.

### Phase 3: The Connection (Month 3)
- [ ] **Backend Integration:** Connect Firebase for global leaderboards and cloud saves.
- [ ] **AI News Feed:** Use an LLM API to generate dynamic, context-aware headlines.
- [ ] **Deployment:** Final Android optimization and Play Store Beta upload.
